I am a Veterinary Medical Researcher with a passion for advancing animal and public health through research and innovation. My academic background combines veterinary medicine, animal biomedical sciences, and molecular epidemiology, with a particular focus on antimicrobial resistance and molecular microbiology.

I earned my Master's degree in Animal Biomedical Sciences (Veterinary Public Health and Epidemiology) from IPB University, Indonesia, graduating with a perfect GPA of 4.0/4.0. My research explored antimicrobial resistance in Escherichia coli isolated from dairy cattle, using molecular and statistical approaches to better understand bacterial resistance patterns.

My goal is to bridge scientific research with practical veterinary applications, contributing to animal health, food safety, and One Health through evidence-based research, collaboration, and continuous learning.
